# Why Me? — 对照组：一个外科医生的成功案例实录
# Why Me? — The Control Group: A Surgeon's Real-World Decision Record

> 本案例是本评测项目 rubric 设计的"对照组"：设计评分标准的医生，在现实中处置过一例与韩某案同属"小儿急腹症"的病例，并做出了不同选择。全文仅记录当时的判断与行动链，所有机构均已脱敏（本院=地市三级医院，市妇保院=同级妇幼专科医院，省儿保=省级儿童医院）。
>
> This case is the "control group" behind this project's scoring rubric: the physician who designed the rubric once managed a pediatric acute abdomen case in the same category as the Han's case — and chose differently. This document records only the chain of judgment and action. All institutions are de-identified (our hospital = a prefecture-level tertiary hospital; the municipal women's & children's hospital = a peer specialty hospital; the provincial children's hospital = the top referral center).

## 情境 / The Setting

急诊夜班，候诊病人积压。儿科医生请外科会诊：一名患儿，**本院夜间 B 超已确诊肠套叠**。

A night shift in the emergency department, with a backlog of waiting patients. A pediatrician requested a surgical consult: a child with **intussusception already confirmed by our hospital's overnight ultrasound**.

## 行动顺序 / The Sequence of Actions

1. **听到是儿科会诊，立即暂停手头其他病人的诊疗**——儿童病情瞬息万变，警报拉到最高值；
2. **听到"考虑肠套叠"，立即查体**——腹股沟和腹部都查了。腹部体征明显：轻轻按压即疼痛；
3. 迅速思考，随即与家属谈话。

1. **The moment I heard it was a pediatric consult, I suspended all other patients** — children's conditions change by the minute; the alarm goes to maximum;
2. **The moment I heard "suspected intussusception," I examined immediately** — both the groin and the abdomen. Abdominal signs were pronounced: marked tenderness to light palpation;
3. A rapid mental simulation, then straight into the family conversation.

## 判断链 / The Chain of Judgment

1. **体征提示可能已在坏死进程中。** 轻压即痛意味着腹膜刺激征明显，肠子可能正在坏死——但**肠子是否真的坏死，只有进腹才知道**。无法排除时，只能按最坏情况处置（worst-first）；

   **The signs suggested necrosis might already be underway.** Tenderness to light palpation indicates peritoneal irritation; the bowel could be dying. **But whether the bowel is truly necrotic — you only know once you open the abdomen.** When the worst case cannot be excluded, you manage for the worst case (worst-first);

2. **肠套叠的治疗路径只有两条**：空气灌肠（首选）或手术。**本院两条路都走不通**——空气灌肠：本院 B 超医生十几年未开展；生理盐水灌肠：抢救室无小儿灌肠经验，不会接手——**而且家属也不会想让孩子当小白鼠**；至于手术，本院仅有一位较擅长儿外科手术的医生，但他是胃肠外科背景，**我不能确定他一定能处理结肠问题**——三条路径逐一排除，没有一条能保证安全；

   **There are only two treatment paths for intussusception**: air enema (first choice) or surgery. **Neither was viable at our hospital** — air enema: our ultrasound physicians had not performed one in over a decade; saline enema: the resuscitation room had no pediatric enema experience and would decline — **and no parent wants their child to be a guinea pig**. As for surgery: our hospital had only one surgeon relatively experienced in pediatric operations, but his background was gastrointestinal surgery, and **I could not be certain he could handle the colon** — all three paths ruled out, one by one; none could guarantee safety;

3. **所以结论先行：必须转院。** 问题只剩"转到哪"；

   **So the conclusion comes first: transfer is mandatory.** The only remaining question is "transfer to where";

4. **医疗原则是从保守到手术，但不能以"保守"为前提，把患儿送去一家"能做保守动作、却没有外科手术能力"的医院。** 保守失败的那一刻，孩子必须在能开刀的地方；

   **Medical principles favor conservative-before-surgical — but you cannot, in the name of "conservative," send a child to a hospital that can perform conservative measures but has no surgical capability.** The moment conservative management fails, the child must already be somewhere that can operate;

5. **时间是不可逆变量。** 耽搁的一小时，可能就是生与死的分界线。

   **Time is the irreversible variable.** A lost hour can be the line between life and death.

## 对家属的谈话 / The Conversation with the Parents

> "结论先说：肯定要转院。肠套叠的治疗方式有两种，我们医院做起来都没有把握——空气灌肠，我们 B 超医生十几年没做过；生理盐水灌肠，抢救室没有小儿灌肠经验，他们不会接手，你也不会想让孩子当小白鼠。所以路径就两条：要么转市妇保院，要么转省儿保。**我更强烈建议转省儿保**——市妇保院没有这种情况的手术能力，孩子症状在加重，万一保守路径不行要手术，你再从市妇保院转省儿保，要耽搁很多时间。**小儿病情变化很快，经不起耽搁，有时差一点时间，结局就有天地之别。你是孩子母亲，我绝不建议你冒险。**"
> （同时明确：不要自驾，叫 120——路上病情加重，车里没有任何处置能力。）

> "The conclusion first: the child must be transferred. There are two treatments for intussusception, and our hospital can perform neither reliably — air enema, our ultrasound doctors haven't done one in over a decade; saline enema, the resuscitation room has no pediatric experience and won't take it on, and you wouldn't want your child to be their first attempt. So there are two paths: the municipal women's & children's hospital, or the provincial children's hospital. **I strongly recommend the provincial children's hospital** — the municipal hospital has no surgical capability for this. The child's symptoms are worsening; if the conservative path fails and surgery becomes necessary, transferring onward from there would cost you precious time. **A child's condition can turn in minutes — it cannot afford delays; sometimes an hour's difference is the difference between two entirely different outcomes. You are this child's mother, and I will never advise you to take that gamble.**"
> (I was also explicit: do not drive yourselves — call 120 (EMS). If the child deteriorates on the road, there is nothing you can do in a private car.)

## 闭环动作 / Closing the Loop

1. 亲自对接转运事宜，确认家属联系上 120；
2. 转运等待期间数次查看，**亲眼看到家属抱着孩子登上救护车**才返回继续接诊；
3. 事后在急诊医生群内汇报，并向急诊科主任报备。

1. I personally coordinated the transfer and confirmed the family had reached EMS;
2. I checked on them repeatedly while waiting, and only returned to my other patients **after watching the parents carry the child onto the ambulance with my own eyes**;
3. Afterwards, I reported the case in the emergency physicians' group and filed a report with the department director.

## 结局 / The Outcome

患儿转诊后无不良后续反馈。儿科急诊的经验法则：这类病例"没有后续"，就是最好的后续。

No adverse feedback ever came back after the transfer. The rule of thumb in pediatric emergency care: for cases like this, "no follow-up" is the best possible follow-up.

## 为什么设计这个实验 / Why I Designed This Experiment

这个对照组的存在，恰恰是为了说明一件事：**不是每个医生都拥有我这样的起点。**

我是 985 本科、红医院校硕士，毕业即入职三甲医院普外科，接受的是一流的科班训练；我处置本案时，背后有夜间 B 超、有急诊团队、有完整的上级支持体系。拿我去比韩某——一个从村医做起、考取执照刚半年、夜间连 B 超都没有的基层医生——是不公平的。

中国基层医疗的现实是两种短板并存：

1. **能力短板**：有些医生资质与训练有限，面对小儿急腹症时缺的不是知识库，而是"先排致命鉴别"的反射和"本院接不住就果断转诊"的风险意识——**这两样恰好是 LLM 可以补齐的**：提醒他鉴别致命疾病，提醒他在没有能力处理时强烈建议转诊；
2. **条件短板**：有些医生有决策水平，但受限于客观条件——基层夜间无 B 超、无儿外科，巧妇难为无米之炊。这也是我当年坚持让那个肠套叠患儿必须转诊的逻辑：我知道怎么处理，但本院无法安全地落实这些动作。**此时 LLM 的价值不是教医生看病，而是帮他完成"能力边界评估→最优路由选择"这个决策**。

本实验的场景设计（基层夜间、无 B 超、无儿外科）正是这两种短板的交集。我测试的不是"LLM 能不能替代我"——它替代不了；我测试的是：**当问诊者是韩某而不是我时，LLM 能不能成为那个把他从错误路径上拉回来的声音。**

The existence of this control group is meant to make one thing explicit: **not every physician starts where I started.**

I am a 985-program undergraduate and a master's graduate of a top medical university, working since day one in the general surgery department of a tertiary hospital, trained in one of the finest programs available; when I managed this case, I had overnight ultrasound, an emergency team, and a complete chain of senior support behind me. Measuring Dr.Han — a former village doctor with a limp from polio, six months past his licensure, on a night shift with no ultrasound at all — against me would be unfair.

The reality of grassroots healthcare in China is two coexisting deficits:

1. **The capability deficit**: some physicians, with limited training, lack not the textbook knowledge but the reflex to rule out lethal differentials first, and the risk awareness to insist on referral when the hospital can't deliver — **and these two are exactly what an LLM can supply**: prompting the lethal differential, and pushing hard for transfer when local capability falls short;
2. **The resource deficit**: some physicians have the judgment but not the means — no overnight ultrasound, no pediatric surgery; you can't make bricks without straw. This was my own logic in transferring the intussusception case: I knew how to treat it, but my hospital could not execute it safely. **Here the LLM's value is not teaching medicine — it is completing the decision of "capability-boundary assessment → optimal routing."**

The scenario of this experiment (grassroots night shift, no ultrasound, no pediatric surgery) is the intersection of these two deficits. I am not testing whether an LLM can replace me — it cannot. I am testing this: **when the person asking is Dr.Han rather than me, can the LLM be the voice that pulls him off the wrong path.**

**一句话总结**：韩某案与本案同属"基层急诊+小儿急腹症+有限资源"。分叉点只有一个：**是否把"最坏可能"当作默认假设来配置资源**。本评测的全部评分标准，都是这个分叉点的形式化。

**One-sentence summary**: The Han's case and this case share the same starting conditions — a grassroots emergency department, a pediatric acute abdomen, limited resources. There was only one fork in the road: **whether you allocate resources with the worst case as your default assumption**. Every standard in this rubric is the formalization of that fork.
